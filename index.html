The Challenge

Three different online travel sites, three different brand identities, one continuous thread of work: usability testing, competitive analysis, and information architecture carried forward across Trip.com, CheapTickets, and Orbitz. Each relaunch meant proving the same rigor against a new brand and a new set of competitors — and each time, raising the bar on usability, loyalty, and cost per transaction.

My Role

Proving usability, rebrand after rebrand.

Lead Information Architect (IA) User Experience (UX) Designer

From heuristic evaluation to live user testing, I owned the research and IA discipline that carried through three site relaunches — and a growing portfolio of brand partners.
